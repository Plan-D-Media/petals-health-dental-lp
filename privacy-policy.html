Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the details you give us when you use a form on this page to book a dental consultation, and what is measured when you visit it. It is an interim notice written by the website team; Petals Health's full privacy policy will replace it.

What we collect

What you type into the form: your name and mobile number and, if you choose to give them, your email address, a preferred date and any message. We also record that you agreed to be contacted, and the wording you agreed to.

With each request we also record how it reached us:

  • the page and form it came from, the time it was sent and a reference number;
  • the page you first arrived on and the website that sent you there, if any;
  • the campaign details in the link you followed (UTM tags, and the Google and Meta ad click identifiers known as gclid and fbclid);
  • your device type (mobile, tablet or desktop) and the size of your screen.

Why we collect it

To contact you about the request you made: to confirm a consultation, call you back, or answer your question. The campaign and device details show us which advertising brings requests and how the page is working. We do not use your details for marketing unless you have separately agreed to that.

Where it goes

Your request is sent through a Google Apps Script to Petals Health's appointment team by email, and stored in a Google spreadsheet. A copy of each request is also emailed to Plan D Media, the agency that runs this page for Petals Health. Google provides the services that carry and store it. Only Petals Health's staff and Plan D Media can open the spreadsheet. Your details are not sold, and are not shared with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Advertising and measurement tags

This page uses Google Tag Manager, which can load advertising and measurement tools from Google and Meta. These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers on your device, and they can record that you visited this page, that you followed an ad, and that you sent a request. Google and Meta handle that information under their own privacy policies.

When a request is delivered, this page tells those tools that it was sent, and passes them its reference number. This page does not pass them your name, mobile number, email address or message.

Maps and directions

The clinic maps on this page are pictures until you choose to open them. If you select "View interactive map", the map loads from Google, and if you select "Get Direction" you go to Google Maps. Google then receives your device's connection details and may set cookies, under its own privacy policy. If you do neither, this page does not load Google Maps.

How long we keep it

For as long as is needed to deal with your request and your care, and as required by law for medical records.

The campaign details are held in your browser until you close the tab, so they can be attached to your request.

If your request cannot be sent, for example because your connection drops, the details you entered are kept in this browser on your device so they can be sent again. They are sent automatically the next time you open this page, and are then removed from your browser. If they have not been sent within 7 days, they are deleted the next time you open the page. If you do not come back to the page, they stay in your browser until you clear its site data, so please do that if you use a shared device.

  • You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.
