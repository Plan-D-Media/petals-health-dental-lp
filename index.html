Frequently Asked Questions

How often should you visit the dentist for a check-up?

It is recommended to visit your dentist at least once every six months for regular check-ups and professional cleanings.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes, dental X-rays are safe, and modern technology ensures minimal radiation exposure. Your dentist will use them only when necessary to diagnose hidden dental issues.

Should my gums be bleeding when I brush?

No - bleeding gums are never normal. They're often an early sign of gingivitis or gum disease.

Can a cavity heal by itself?

Unfortunately, no. Once decay sets in, it needs professional treatment to stop it from spreading. The earlier you treat it, the simpler the procedure.

How do I know if I need a root canal?

Persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, or a pimple on the gums are common warning signs of an infected tooth pulp. Visit us before it gets worse.
