Is a root canal painful?

A root canal treats the pain rather than causing it: the toothache comes from infected pulp inside the tooth, and the treatment removes it. Afterwards, mild discomfort or sensitivity for the first day is common, and soreness over the next few days can be managed with over-the-counter pain relief. If the pain gets worse instead of better, call us.

How many visits will my dental treatment take?

It depends on the treatment: a check-up is usually one visit, a root canal usually two to three visits, a crown about two visits, and clear aligners or braces are counted in months rather than visits.

Petals Health Dental Clinic plans treatment in stages: a consultation and X-rays first, then the treatment itself, then follow-up visits as needed. These are rough guides rather than promises, and your dentist will confirm after examining you.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes. Dental X-rays are safe, and modern technology ensures minimal radiation exposure. Your dentist will use them only when necessary to diagnose hidden dental issues. Petals Health Dental Clinic has dental X-ray facilities on site, so you don't need to go elsewhere for them.

How do I know if I need a root canal?

Persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, pain when biting, pain that lingers after something hot or cold, swelling, a tooth that is darkening, a small pimple-like bump on the gum, or a bad taste are common warning signs of an infected tooth pulp. An examination and a dental X-ray show whether the pulp is infected.

Not every toothache needs a root canal. Only an examination and X-ray can confirm.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding gums aren't normal: they are often an early sign of gingivitis or gum disease. Don't stop brushing. Brush gently and floss daily. If the bleeding lasts more than a week, book a check-up. Early gum disease is treated with scaling, and advanced cases may need deeper treatment under the dentist's guidance.

Can a cavity heal on its own?

No. Once decay sets in, it needs professional treatment to stop it from spreading, and the earlier it's treated, the simpler the procedure. Cavities are treated with tooth-coloured or silver fillings.

How often should I go to the dentist for a check-up?

At least once every six months for a check-up and professional cleaning. A check-up at Petals Health Dental Clinic includes an examination of your teeth and gums and a professional cleaning to remove plaque, tartar and stains. Your dentist may suggest a different interval based on your needs.
